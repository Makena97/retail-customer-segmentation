# BrightCart Public Trust Statement

## Our Commitment

BrightCart uses data analytics and machine learning to support better customer-retention decisions. The repeat-purchase prediction system estimates the likelihood that an existing customer will purchase again within 90 days.

The model is intended to support human decision-making rather than replace human judgement.

## Responsible Use of Customer Data

BrightCart is committed to using customer information only for legitimate and defined purposes.

The prediction model uses purchasing-behaviour features such as purchase recency, frequency, monetary value, average order value, quantity purchased, product variety and customer tenure.

Direct customer identifiers are not used as predictive model features.

Data should be protected through appropriate access controls, pseudonymization, retention controls and security practices.

## Transparency

A model prediction represents an estimate rather than certainty about an individual customer's future behaviour.

BrightCart documents:

- the model's intended purpose;
- the information used to generate predictions;
- model performance and limitations;
- explainability results;
- fairness assessments; and
- monitoring and governance procedures.

## Fairness

BrightCart is committed to monitoring whether the model performs differently across relevant customer groups.

The current geographic fairness assessment compares UK and Non-UK customers. Because the Non-UK validation sample is relatively small, these results are treated as exploratory rather than conclusive.

Fairness concerns should be investigated and documented before corrective action is taken.

## Explainability

BrightCart uses explainability techniques such as SHAP to understand the factors influencing model predictions.

Explanations are intended to help stakeholders understand and investigate model behaviour. They should not be interpreted as proof that a feature caused a customer's purchasing behaviour.

## Human Oversight

The model should not independently make decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects on individuals.

Human oversight is required when model performance deteriorates, unusual predictions occur, fairness concerns arise, significant data drift is detected, or the model's intended use changes.

## Monitoring and Accountability

Model performance, data drift, prediction drift and fairness should be monitored throughout the system lifecycle.

Significant issues should be documented, investigated and escalated through the BrightCart incident-response process.

Where a model can no longer operate reliably or responsibly, it may be suspended, replaced or decommissioned.

## Continuous Improvement

Responsible AI is an ongoing process.

BrightCart's model, monitoring controls and governance practices should therefore be reviewed as new data becomes available and as business, technical and regulatory conditions change.
